# Section

**Definition.** One chapter of [[DT-020-req42|req42]] or [[DT-021-arc42|arc42]] as the [[DT-018-metamodel|metamodel]] declares it, for example R3 or A2. A section holds the pages of one or more wiki folders.

**Distinguish from.** arc42 calls its chapters sections too. "Building block" is a page type of arc42 section 5, never a word for a section.
