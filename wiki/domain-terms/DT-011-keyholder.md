# Keyholder

**Definition.** The one person in a [[DT-010-session|session]] who may change the [[DT-012-view|view]] and disconnect the others. The stakeholder page is [[STK-023-keyholder]].

**Distinguish from.** The workshop facilitator leads the workshop. The Keyholder holds the rights in the dashboard. They can be two people.
