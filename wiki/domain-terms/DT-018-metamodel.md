# Metamodel

**Definition.** The description of the page types, their fields, relations, [[DT-006-section|sections]] and [[DT-004-projection|projections]], in `_system/metamodel/`. [[DT-019-systems42|Systems42]] defines it. The [[DT-001-dashboard|dashboard]] reads it.
