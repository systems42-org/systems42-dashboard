# View

**Definition.** The choice of [[DT-005-area|areas]] and [[DT-006-section|sections]] that a [[DT-010-session|session]] shows. It lasts until the [[DT-001-dashboard|dashboard]] stops.

**Distinguish from.** An arc42 view (building block view, runtime view) is a section of the architecture, not this choice.
