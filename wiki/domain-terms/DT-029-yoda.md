# Yoda

**Definition.** The nickname the [[DT-001-dashboard|dashboard]] gives to the first [[DT-011-keyholder|Keyholder]] of a [[DT-010-session|session]]. The header greets that person with "Welcome, Yoda".

**In context.** The name is a joke: Yoda is the Jedi Master in Star Wars, and the first Keyholder holds the rights over the session. A later Keyholder keeps their own nickname.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-011-keyholder|Keyholder]] is the role. Yoda is only the name of its first holder.
