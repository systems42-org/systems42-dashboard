# Systems42 wiki

**Definition.** A repository that holds requirements and architecture as typed [[DT-015-markdown|Markdown]] pages under `wiki/`, with sources under `raw/` and the method files under `_system/`.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-003-systems42-derivative|Systems42 derivative]]: every Systems42 wiki is a derivative. "Wiki" names the content, "derivative" names where the repository comes from.
