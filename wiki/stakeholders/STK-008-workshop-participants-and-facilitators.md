# Workshop participants and facilitators

**Snapshot.** Participants scan the QR code and read on their own phone or laptop, with no account and no installation. Many know neither the method nor its vocabulary. The facilitator leads the workshop and projects the QR code from `/join`. They hold the control rights only if they are the [[STK-023-keyholder|Keyholder]].

**Goals.**
- Look at the same requirements together, each on their own device.
- Get the method's terms explained.

**Concerns / pains.**
- Unfamiliar method and vocabulary.
