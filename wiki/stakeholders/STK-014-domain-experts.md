# Domain experts

**Snapshot.** They are asked whether a term or a process is right. They read and review the glossary and activity models, and they can remove an "Assumption" badge from a definition. They do not know the Systems42 method and should not have to.

**Goals.**
- Confirm or correct terms and processes.

**Concerns / pains.**
- Having to learn the method before they can help.

**Interactions.** None recorded yet.
