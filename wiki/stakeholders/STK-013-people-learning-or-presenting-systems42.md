# People learning or presenting Systems42

**Snapshot.** Trainers, consultants and speakers. `/help` and `/metamodel` explain the method, and the dashboard is what they put on the screen to show it. People who consider using Systems42 belong here too: the public website is made for them ([[FR-056-a-public-website-for-the-dashboard]]).

**Goals.**
- Judge the dashboard before installing anything.
- Show the method convincingly. This group is the strongest reason for the dashboard to look attractive.

**Concerns / pains.**
- The dashboard should help readers understand its purpose and learn the Systems42 method ([[QR-005-the-dashboard-supports-learning]]).
- Malicious markup can arrive in the sources a wiki captures. The dashboard must keep it inert for workshop participants and public-demo visitors ([[QR-006-hostile-wiki-content-is-inert]]).

**Interactions.** None recorded yet.
