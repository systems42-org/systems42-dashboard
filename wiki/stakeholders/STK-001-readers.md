# Readers

**Snapshot.** Everyone who reads the wiki through the dashboard in everyday work. Each alias names one kind of reader.

**Goals.**
- See what is agreed, what is still open, and why.

**Concerns / pains.**
- Testers: no page collects the acceptance criteria across requirements.
- Domain experts should not have to learn the method before they can confirm or correct a term.
- Newcomers: nothing leads them through the wiki.
- Reviewers cannot fix what they find, because the dashboard never writes to the wiki ([[CON-002-dashboard-never-writes-to-the-wiki]]).
