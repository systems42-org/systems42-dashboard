# Workshop facilitators

**Snapshot.** The person who leads a workshop. They project the QR code full-size from `/join` and work through the five top sections a workshop keeps returning to. They hold the dashboard's control rights only if they are also the Keyholder ([[STK-023-keyholder]]). Sometimes they also run the dashboard ([[STK-010-people-who-run-the-dashboard]]).

**Goals.**
- Show the requirements to a room while participants follow on their own devices.
