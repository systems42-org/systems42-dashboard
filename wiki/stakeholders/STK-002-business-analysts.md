# Business analysts

**Snapshot.** Business analysts check that the requirements match how the business works. In many organisations they are the same person as the requirements engineer ([[STK-003-requirements-engineers]]). This page stays separate because the interest differs: the business itself, versus the state of the requirements.

**Goals.**
- Check that goals, processes and terms match how the business works.
