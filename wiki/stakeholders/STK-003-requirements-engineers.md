# Requirements engineers

**Snapshot.** Requirements engineers review what has been recorded. They often are the same person as the business analyst ([[STK-002-business-analysts]]).

**Goals.**
- Spot gaps, contradictions and claims without a source.

**Concerns / pains.**
- They cannot fix anything they find here. The dashboard never writes to the wiki, so every correction happens elsewhere.
