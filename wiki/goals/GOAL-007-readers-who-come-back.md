# Readers who come back

## Objective

> follows [[PAM]]

*From the vision: "The pages are a pleasure to read, so people come back."*

- **Purpose.** Readers return to the dashboard on their own, because reading it is easier than any alternative.
- **Advantage.** A wiki that is read gets corrected. A wiki nobody opens goes stale, and the effort of recording it is lost.
- **Metric.** Share of the project team that opens the dashboard at least once a week without being asked, found by asking the team, since the dashboard does not track readers. Proposed target: 80 %.

> [!note]
> The target is a proposal. Nobody has agreed it yet. No baseline has been measured.

**Parent.** [[GOAL-001-trusted-answers]].

## Impact

- **Addressed stakeholders:** everyone who reads the dashboard, that is the Reader group and the Keyholder.
- **Served by:** projected from the FR backlinks.
