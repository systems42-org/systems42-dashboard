# Requirements with their reasons and their checks

## Objective

> follows [[PAM]]

*From the vision: "They see what is required and why."*

- **Purpose.** For every requirement the reader sees what is demanded, which goal it serves, how important it is and how it is checked.
- **Advantage.** Product owners decide scope with the reasons in front of them. Development teams build what was meant. Testers know what counts as passed.
- **Metric.** Time a product owner or tester needs to list the requirements of the next release, each with its goal and its acceptance criteria. Proposed target: under 2 minutes.

> [!note]
> The target is a proposal. Nobody has agreed it yet. No baseline has been measured.
