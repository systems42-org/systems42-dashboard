# Trusted answers about requirements and architecture

## Vision

> follows the Geoffrey Moore format

For **everyone who works on a system and has a question about its requirements or
architecture**, the **Systems42 dashboard** is a **read-only view of the Systems42
wiki** that **gives the answer faster than asking a colleague, and shows where it
came from and how firm it is**.

Anyone with a question about a system's requirements or architecture finds the
answer in the Systems42 dashboard faster than by asking a colleague. They see what
is required and why, how the system is built to meet it, and what has been decided.
They can tell where each answer came from and how firm it is. The pages are a
pleasure to read, so people come back.

## Impact

- **Addressed stakeholders:** everyone who reads the dashboard, that is the Reader
  group and the Keyholder.
