# Source and status on every statement

## Objective

> follows [[PAM]]

*From the vision: "They can tell where each answer came from and how firm it is."*

- **Purpose.** Wherever the dashboard shows a statement from the wiki, it displays its status and source visibly and lets the reader restrict views to agreed statements only. Recording status and provenance in the wiki belongs to Systems42 ([[CON-004-the-dashboard-shows-the-metamodel-it-does-not-define-it]]). The dashboard's job is to make them impossible to miss.
- **Advantage.** Nobody acts on a draft believing it is agreed. Product owners see what is still open. Requirements engineers spot unsourced claims. Auditors trace a requirement without interviewing anyone.
- **Metric.** Share of 10 statements shown in the dashboard for which a reader correctly names the status and the source. Proposed target: 10 of 10.

> [!note]
> The target is a proposal. Nobody has agreed it yet. No baseline has been measured.
