# Source and status on every statement

## Objective

> follows [[PAM]]

*From the vision: "They can tell where each answer came from and how firm it is."*

- **Purpose.** Wherever a statement appears, the reader sees its status and its source, and can restrict any view to what is agreed.
- **Advantage.** Nobody acts on a draft believing it is agreed. Product owners see what is still open. Requirements engineers spot unsourced claims. Auditors trace a requirement without interviewing anyone.
- **Metric.** Share of 10 statements for which a reader correctly names the status and the source. Proposed target: 10 of 10.

> [!note]
> The target is a proposal. Nobody has agreed it yet. No baseline has been measured.
