# The dashboard supports learning

**Q42 dimensions.** Usable.

The dashboard helps readers understand what it does and learn how Systems42 works, without requiring a colleague to explain it.

**Scenario (Q42 quality scenario).**
- **Source.** A reader new to the dashboard and Systems42 ([[STK-001-readers]] or [[STK-013-people-learning-or-presenting-systems42]]).
- **Stimulus.** Tries to understand the dashboard and the Systems42 method.
- **Environment.** First visit, without a presenter or colleague explaining the pages.
- **Response.** The dashboard makes its purpose and method understandable through its views and learning material.
- **Measure.** After at most 10 minutes, at least 4 of 5 test readers can explain what the dashboard does and name the five Systems42 loops with their purpose.

**Applies to.** [[FR-010-show-the-method]], [[FR-011-explain-the-five-loops]], [[FR-012-guided-path-through-the-wiki]], [[FR-066-see-the-architecture-views]] and [[FR-068-read-the-metamodel-as-a-reference]].
**Tensions.** None known.
