**Affects.** [[FR-011-explain-the-five-loops]], which explains Clarify as one of the five current Systems42 loops.

**Context / evidence.**
- Clarify is pieced together from the grilling step of `_system/workflows/capture.md` (step 6) and the handover at the end of `inspect.md`.
- A starting point, proposed by Claude and not agreed: open questions go to a human, who decides. The answer flows back into the pages. Whatever stays unanswered is kept as an Issue.

**Options.**
1. Take the starting point as it is.
2. The Systems42 team writes its own definition.

**Resolution.** Systems42 now defines Clarify in [[clarify]]. The dashboard's five-loop explanation can describe the loop and its place in the method ([[SRC-056-closing-round-on-recovery-report]]).
