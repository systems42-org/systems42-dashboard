# Show the method

## Epic: Summary

As a [[STK-013-people-learning-or-presenting-systems42|person presenting Systems42]] I want **to show on a real wiki how Systems42 works, in a few minutes**, so that **[[STK-022-prospective-adopters|prospective adopters]] judge the method from a working example instead of slides**.

**PROBLEM / GOAL**

A presenter needs to show that a raw source becomes typed, linked pages with source and status, traced from goal to requirement to architecture. The audience also needs to understand the loops that keep the wiki alive, not only their result.

**Success metric(s).** The metric of [[GOAL-002-show-what-systems42-can-do]], once agreed. Proposed: a presenter who did not build the wiki gives the demo in 10 minutes or less, without leaving the dashboard, in 3 of 3 trial runs.

**Scope.** in: explaining the method on the wiki at hand · out: teaching material and slides outside the dashboard.
