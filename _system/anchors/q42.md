# Q42 — quality scenarios

The shape of the scenario on every quality requirement, after the arc42 quality
model **Q42** ([quality.arc42.org](https://quality.arc42.org)): an ISO 25010
attribute made concrete and testable by one scenario. Q42 is the pragmatic
choice here; the wiki never calls these "ATAM scenarios".

- **Source** — who or what generates the stimulus (a stakeholder, a system, the environment).
- **Stimulus** — the event that arrives.
- **Environment** — the operating conditions at that moment (normal load, failure, release day …).
- **Response** — what the system does.
- **Measure** — the quantified, verifiable threshold that makes the requirement testable.

On a page the scenario sits under the heading **Scenario (Q42 quality scenario)**,
one bullet per part, labelled exactly as above (the dashboard reads them by label).
Quality.arc42.org also collects example requirements per quality attribute —
a good place to look for a measure before inventing one.

## Checklist (audit / grill)
- [ ] All five parts are present; the Source names a stakeholder or system, not "the user" in general where a specific one is meant.
- [ ] The Measure is a number or a pass/fail check someone can run; without it set `testable: false`.
- [ ] The Environment says under which conditions the Measure holds.
- [ ] No technology in the scenario — it belongs on the architecture pages (DR-004).
