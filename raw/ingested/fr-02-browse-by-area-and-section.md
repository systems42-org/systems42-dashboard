# Browse by area and section

Extracted on 2026-11-10 from the dashboard code and the goals. Rewritten on 2026-11-11 as needs, at Gernot Starke's request. Nobody has agreed this requirement yet.

**Requirement.** A reader who does not know the right word reaches any page in three steps: area, section, page.

**Serves.** GOAL-003 Answers without asking a colleague.

**For.** Newcomers, workshop participants, everyone who reads the dashboard.

## Needs

- **Follow the project's metamodel.** In the real Systems42 the metamodel is configurable, so a project or product adapts it to its needs and may leave out parts of requirements, architecture or shared content. Areas and sections follow that configuration: a part the project left out appears nowhere, neither on the first page nor in navigation, counts or lists. The three steps work the same for every configuration.
- **Start with orientation.** The first page shows the areas (requirements, architecture, shared content), how complete and mature the wiki is, what changed lately and which questions are open.
- **See a section at a glance.** Each section shows what it holds before the reader opens it: how many pages, a few of them by name, and how mature they are.
- **Same name everywhere.** A section carries the same name wherever it appears, so the reader recognises the page a link leads to.
- **Sort lists by what matters to the reader.** Every section list sorts by any of its columns, and each row opens its page.
- **Know where you are.** Every page shows its place: area, section, page. One click leads back up.
- **Share what you see.** A filtered or sorted list has its own address, so a reader can send exactly that view to someone else.
