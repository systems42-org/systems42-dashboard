# Guided path through the wiki

Proposed on 2026-11-11 by Claude, at Gernot Starke's request. Nobody has agreed this requirement yet.

**Requirement.** A newcomer or a demo audience follows a guided path through the wiki, step by step, from the vision through goals and requirements to architecture and open issues, without knowing which page to open next.

**Serves.** GOAL-003 Answers without asking a colleague, GOAL-002 Show what Systems42 can do.

**For.** Newcomers, workshop participants, people learning or presenting Systems42, prospective adopters.
