# See what is still open

Extracted on 2026-11-10 from the dashboard code and the goals. Rewritten on 2026-11-12 as needs, at Gernot Starke's request. Nobody has agreed this requirement yet.

**Requirement.** A reader knows which questions about the system are still open, overall and for the part of the wiki they are reading, and finds a single issue quickly.

**Serves.** GOAL-006 Source and status on every statement.

**For.** Product owners, requirements engineers, architects, auditors and quality assurance.

## Needs

- **Know what is open.** A reader sees how many issues are open and which ones, without opening each page.
- **Issues have a category.** Every issue belongs to one category, so readers see which part of the work it touches: requirements, architecture, shared content, content of the wiki itself, organisation, or Systems42 itself (its metamodel, templates and loops). A risk is a kind of issue, not a category.
- **Search issues.** A reader finds an issue by words from its title or text.
- **Filter issues.** A reader narrows the list by status, category and severity.
- **Sort issues.** A reader sorts the list by several columns, such as status, then severity.
- **Open issues where they matter.** A section shows how many open issues touch its pages, and that number leads to exactly those issues. A page shows the issues that point at it.
- **Know who answers.** A reader sees who is expected to answer an issue. No due dates: the wiki is not a project management tool.
- **Read only.** Issues are always created and changed in the wiki. The dashboard only shows them and never creates or changes one.
