# Long-term vision of the Systems42 dashboard

Chosen on 2026-11-10 from three alternatives. The draft of 2026-11-09 with all
three is in the git history.

> Anyone with a question about a system's requirements or architecture finds the
> answer in the Systems42 dashboard faster than by asking a colleague. They see
> what is required and why, how the system is built to meet it, and what has been
> decided. They can tell where each answer came from and how firm it is. The pages
> are a pleasure to read, so people come back.
